# User guide — Vendors

Keep supplier and support details in one place.

## Add a vendor

1. Select **Add record**.
2. Enter the vendor name and optionally the sales or support email, phone, website, customer account number and support contact.
3. Select **Create record**.

## Tips

- Vendors are shared across the workspace; they do not have to belong to an organization.
- Choose the vendor on a purchase order to keep a record of where devices came from.
