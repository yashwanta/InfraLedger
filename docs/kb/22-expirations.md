# User guide — Expirations

Stay ahead of renewals, warranties and documentation reviews.

## What appears here

Any record with an expiration or review date that is overdue or due within **90 days**, including:

- Warranty expirations on assets
- Password rotation dates
- License and agreement renewals
- Domain and certificate expirations
- Article and runbook review dates

## Using the page

- Items are sorted by due date and show the days remaining or the days overdue.
- Search by record or organization name, and click a row to open the record.
- The sidebar shows how many items are due.

## Notes

Dates use the workspace time zone and come from the dates you record.
