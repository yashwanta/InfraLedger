# Getting started with InfraLedger

InfraLedger keeps a complete, connected record of the IT you support: devices, networks, credentials, racks, diagrams, procedures, licenses, renewals and purchases, organized by organization (client).

## First steps

1. Create an **Organization** for each company or site you support.
2. Add **Locations** and **Contacts** for that organization.
3. Document devices in **Asset inventory**, then place them in **Racks** and give them **IP addresses**.
4. Store credentials in the **Password vault** and link them to the systems they unlock.
5. Write **Knowledge base** articles and **Runbooks** for repeatable work.
6. Add renewal dates to licenses, domains, certificates and agreements so **Expirations** can warn you.

## Finding your way

- The left sidebar lists every page, grouped into Workspace, Documentation, Infrastructure, Operations and Management.
- The **organization selector** in the top bar limits every page to one organization. Choose **All organizations** to see everything.
- Press **Ctrl K** (or **Cmd K**) to search the whole workspace.
- Click any row or card to open the record. From there you can edit it, favorite it, attach files, comment, view version history and see linked documentation.

## Roles

- **Admin:** full access, manages users, API tokens and the audit log.
- **Editor:** creates and edits records in assigned organizations, and can reveal vault secrets.
- **Viewer:** read-only; cannot reveal secrets.
- **Client:** sees only assigned organizations and records marked **Client visible**.

Each page has its own guide in this knowledge base, named **User guide — page name**.
