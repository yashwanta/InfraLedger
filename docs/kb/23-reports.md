# User guide — Reports

Understand coverage, lifecycle and workload at a glance.

## Figures

- **Inventory value:** total recorded purchase cost of assets.
- **Open purchase orders:** orders that are not yet received or cancelled.
- **Articles needing review:** articles whose review date is today or past.
- **License seats available:** purchased minus assigned seats.

## Charts

Asset lifecycle status, inventory by device type, records per organization and rack utilization.

## Export

Select **Export report** to download the figures, assets, purchases and expirations as JSON. Reports follow the organization selector.
