# User guide — Ports & cabling

Document switch ports, uplinks and physical connections between devices.

## Add a port

1. Select **Add record** and choose the organization.
2. Choose the device and enter the port identifier, for example `Gi1/0/1`.
3. Optionally choose the connected device and port, link speed, VLAN or trunk, and cable ID.
4. Set the status (Connected, Available, Disabled) and select **Create record**.

## Tips

- Labelling cables with a **Cable ID** makes physical tracing much faster.
- Open a device in Asset inventory to see its ports under **Linked documentation**.
