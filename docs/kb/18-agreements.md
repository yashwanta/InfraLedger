# User guide — Agreements

Record contracts, support agreements and their renewal terms.

## Add an agreement

1. Select **Add record** and choose the organization.
2. Enter the provider, contract owner, annual value, renewal date, notice period in days and status (Active, Pending, Expired, Cancelled).
3. Attach the signed contract from the record's **Attachments** section (up to 5 MB).

## Tips

Check the **Notice period** against the renewal date on the Expirations page so you can cancel or renegotiate in time.
